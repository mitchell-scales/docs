Agents can answer questions, debug issues, reason across systems, and work inside workflows. They use your reports, models, datasets, journeys, tasks, and history to give better answers — grounded in the same context your team relies on, not just a chat window.

## How It Works

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Ask the business anything" icon="comment-question">
    Agents answer analytical, operational, customer, and debugging questions because they're connected to the full business context.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Reports, models, tasks, notes, and data together" icon="layer-group">
    Agents can use reports, datasets, models, journeys, workflows, table context, prior tasks, and meeting notes to understand what the user is really asking.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Runs natively on Codex" icon="terminal">
    Polyform is built CLI-first, so the AI opens Codex and does the work as if you would.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Understands how your company thinks" icon="brain">
    Agents learn from your definitions, prior analysis, reports, workflows, and operating context, so answers match how your business actually works.
  </Card>
</Columns>
