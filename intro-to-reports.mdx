## Reports

Dashboards with charts, tables, and narrative context.

## Table Views

Focused dataset tables shared with report permissions — for looking at the same table repeatedly without building a full report around it.

---

## Create

Clicking **Create** lets you choose one of two types.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Report" icon="chart-line">
    Build a new report
  </Card>

  <Card title="Table View" icon="table">
    Start from a data table
  </Card>
</Columns>

---

## Row Actions

Each report or table view has an options menu (⋮):

- **Share** — share access to the report or table view.
- **Duplicate** — duplicates the report or table view.
- **Delete** — deletes the report or table view.

---

## Sharing Reports

Reports default to **Private** when created. There are two ways to share one.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="From the index page" icon="ellipsis-vertical">
    Press the three-dot menu on a report's row and choose **Share**.
  </Card>

  <Card title="From inside a report" icon="arrow-up-from-bracket">
    Open the report, and press **Share** at the top, next to **Edit**.
  </Card>
</Columns>

Both entry points open the same sharing panel, where you can **add a team** or **copy the link**.

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 2026 07 30 At 14 32 08](/images/Screenshot-2026-07-30-at-14.32.08.png "Screenshot 2026 07 30 At 14 32 08")
</Frame>

<Note>
  Every workspace has a built-in **Everyone** team. Sharing with Everyone makes the report visible to every member of Polyform on that site.
</Note>

<Tip>
  A user's access level, set in Settings, can also grant visibility into reports beyond what's been explicitly shared with them — some access types can see other users' reports regardless of sharing.
</Tip>
