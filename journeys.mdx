---
title: "Journeys"
description: "Search for a person and inspect the sequence, gaps, and recency of their activity across every stream."
---

The Journeys page lets you search for a customer and watch each activity stream play out, so you can inspect the sequence, gaps, and recency of their journey.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a stream">
    Choose which stream to search within — for example, User or Doctor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter an identifier">
    Search by the customer's ID or email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Limit activities (optional)">
    Narrow the journey down to specific activities rather than loading everything.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a time range (optional)">
    Choose a preset range — 31 days, This Week, This Month, This Year, 12 Months, 24 Months, All Time — or set a **Custom** range.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View the journey">
    The full sequence of activities for that customer is returned, in order.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Limiting activities is useful when you only care about a subset of the journey. For example, investigating an email campaign might mean limiting to just Received Email, Opened Email, Clicked Email, and Started Session.
</Tip>
