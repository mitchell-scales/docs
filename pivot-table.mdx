---
title: "Pivot Table"
description: "Aggregate metrics across dimensions, either cumulatively or over time."
---

A pivot table aggregates metrics across dimensions, in one of two modes.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Cumulative" icon="chart-pie">
    Select multiple metrics and dimensions, with full filter and compare support. Use the **Configure** button on the table itself to switch individual metrics and dimensions between rows and columns.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Over Time" icon="chart-line">
    Select a granularity and the table shows your metrics at each time period, also supporting multiple metrics and comparisons.
  </Card>
</Columns>
